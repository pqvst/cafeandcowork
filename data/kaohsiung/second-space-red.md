review:
  en: |
    The second and much bigger Second Space, opened in May 2026 on Qixian 1st Road. Same 24/7 self-service model as the Central Park branch — you sign up in the app, register your face, and the door lets you in at any hour. No staff, no cash, and payment is card or LINE Pay only.

    The ground floor is a takeaway coffee counter, so the entire working space is the second floor and none of the cafe noise reaches it. Upstairs you get open coworking seating, dedicated desks, three phone booths and a meeting room — all with noise-cancelling walls — plus an event room with a large projector that doubles as a podcast recording space. Total occupancy is 45. Everything is included in the entrance fee with no paid add-ons, which is genuinely unusual for a day-pass space; booths and meeting rooms are normally metered elsewhere.

    WiFi is 500 Mbps on Wi-Fi 7, and there are monitors at the desks. Big windows keep it bright, though the view is just a busy road rather than anything scenic. Food is the weak spot — coffee and snacks downstairs, no kitchen — but there's a 7-Eleven and a Starbucks right next door, and the Liuhe night market is about ten minutes away.

    At NT$400/day it's noticeably pricier than the NT$250 Central Park branch, though the booths, meeting room and event space come with it rather than on top. Nearest MRT is Xinyi Elementary School (orange line), about a 6-minute walk; Formosa Boulevard is around 10.
