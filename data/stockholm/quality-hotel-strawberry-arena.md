The hotel lobby of Quality Hotel Strawberry Arena features a corner for working/hanging out which is very empty in the mornings. They have a bar in the evenings but it's not open during the day. This means there's no food or drinks available during daytime, however the Mall of Scandinavia is next door and has a lot of options. The WiFi is fast and reliable and there's a few power outlets along the walls.
