A small café which seems to be a collaboration with Volvo next door since the café connects directly to their showroom. The coffee was excellent featuring special beans replaced almost daily. The place was completely empty on a Monday morning despite the very central location. There is a guest WiFi provided by Volvo however it required signup so I opted not to use it. They don't really have anything to eat apart from a cake so you will need to head out for lunch.
