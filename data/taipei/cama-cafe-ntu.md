A branch of Cama cafe near NTU track field. Coffee is decent, you can get some sandwiches alongside. The window is big and let lots of natural sunlight in. It is in front a You-bike station so access is pretty easy despite it being between two stations. AC was bit too cold for me at the back but if you move closer to the entrance, it was very comfortable. Power outlet is available for most of the seats.
