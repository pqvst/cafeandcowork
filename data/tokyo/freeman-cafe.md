A nice cafe/restaurant close to Shibuya and Harajuku. Interesting drinks and a nice variety of cooked food, although the menu is smaller and less interesting than it used to be. Power available by most seats. WiFi speed is fine, but it can be a bit flaky at peak hours. I wouldn’t go here for downloading huge files. Note that there is a time-limit when it's crowded and I guess they won't ask you to leave unless there's a queue to get in.
