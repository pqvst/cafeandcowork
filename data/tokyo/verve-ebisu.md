Verve's second Ebisu location, about a 5 minute walk from Ebisu station (separate from the Yebisu Garden Place store). Large shared table and stools on the ground floor, plus tables and window counter seats with a view of the street on the 2nd floor. Menu with espresso drinks, single origin pour-overs, waffles, granola, and press sandwiches. No WiFi and very few power outlets, so come with a charged laptop and your own connection.
