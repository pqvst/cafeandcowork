Spacious cafe on the 4th floor of the Aoyama Oval Building, about a 4 minute walk from Omotesando station. It's attached to the goodroom head office, but open to everyone. Stylish interior, but there are no windows or natural light. Very quiet with plenty of free seats and fast WiFi (100 Mbps). Decent drink selection with good coffee, and some nice looking cakes. Very few power outlets, so come with a charged laptop. Last order is at 17:30.
